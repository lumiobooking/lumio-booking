ABOUT LUMIO BOOKING
Lumio Booking (lumiobooking.com) is an appointment scheduling platform built for independent beauty and wellness businesses: nail salons, lash studios, spas and massage centres in the United States, Canada and Australia. Each merchant gets its own booking page with its real service menu (names, durations and prices), its staff, its opening hours and live availability. Clients can book 24/7 and receive an instant confirmation.

1) MERCHANTS USING LINKS PROVIDED BY OUR SYSTEM: 28

How the links are provided:
- Each merchant connects its own Google Business Profile to Lumio Booking with Google sign-in (OAuth) from inside our platform.
- Our system then creates the booking link on that profile through the Google Business Profile Place Actions API, as an APPOINTMENT place action link marked as preferred. Nothing is added by hand.
- Each link opens the merchant's own booking page on our platform at lumiobooking.com/<merchant>/gbp, where the client picks a service, a time and (optionally) a technician and completes the booking.
- The links are re-checked by our system every night, so a profile that changes is corrected automatically.
